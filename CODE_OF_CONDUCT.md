# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be straight with people. Argue about the work, not the person. Assume the other
party has a reason you have not heard yet, and ask.

## Specifically

**Fine, and encouraged:**

- Saying a rule is wrong, a number is wrong, or a design is wrong — directly.
  These tools exist to be argued with; several rules are in their current form
  because someone pushed back.
- Saying "I do not understand this" or "this reads as condescending".
- Declining to do work, or closing your own issue.

**Not fine:**

- Personal attacks, harassment, or making someone's identity the subject.
- Sustained dismissiveness toward someone learning.
- Bad-faith arguing — moving the goalposts, misrepresenting what someone said,
  or relitigating a settled decision without new information.

## Enforcement

Report to **erwin.lejeune15@gmail.com**. Reports stay private.

Responses run from a request to change behaviour, through a warning, to a block.
I will say which one is happening and why, rather than silently disappearing
someone. If you think a call was wrong, say so — you will get a real answer.

There is one maintainer here, so this is a commitment about conduct rather than a
committee process.
