# Support

**A question about how something works** — open a Discussion or an issue on the
repository. Questions are useful signal; if something needed asking, the docs
were unclear.

**A bug** — open an issue with the version, the command you ran, and the output.
For `graphlint`, `preflight` or `authsweep`, `--format json` output is the most
useful thing you can attach.

**A rule that misfires on your codebase** — turn it off in `.graphlintrc.json`
and open an issue naming the architecture it misreads. That is far more useful
than a workaround.

**A security issue** — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Not a public issue.

**Commercial support or consulting** — erwin.lejeune15@gmail.com.

These are side projects maintained by one person. Response is usually within a
few days; it is occasionally a couple of weeks. Nothing here has an SLA, and
pretending otherwise would be worse than saying so.
